Learn moreHow does it work?
▼

AssetAlign matches your employees with a vetted financial advisor and provides them with a personalized financial plan — completely free. As employees work with their advisors and grow their assets, AssetAlign generates revenue through the platform. A portion of that revenue is returned directly to your company every year as the Employer Flex Fund, and a portion goes back to employees as Employee Rewards. No fees. No catch. Just a benefit that pays for itself — and then some.

TransparencySounds too good to be true — what's the catch?
▼

Think of it like your credit card rewards. You earn points and cash back for free — not because the bank is being generous, but because merchants fund the model. AssetAlign works the same way. We've built a three-sided marketplace where financial advisors generate revenue through the platform if your employees choose to engage with them. A portion of that revenue flows back to both the employer and the employee automatically. No tricks. No hidden fees. Just a model that aligns everyone's incentives.
